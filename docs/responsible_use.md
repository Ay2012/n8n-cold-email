# Responsible Use

This workflow is for careful, reviewed outreach drafts. It should not be used for spam, scraping, or bulk blasting.

## Guardrails

- Use verified contacts only.
- Respect unsubscribe requests and no-contact requests.
- Do not misrepresent yourself.
- Do not invent personalization.
- Review drafts before sending.
- Send in small batches.
- Follow Gmail and provider limits.
- Follow applicable laws and platform terms.
- Do not use this to spam people.
- Keep private data out of GitHub.

## Practical checklist

Before sending any message, confirm:

- The contact is relevant.
- The company details are accurate.
- The email is honest and specific.
- The ask is low-pressure.
- The draft does not include invented claims.
- Any attachment is intended for that recipient.
